# Email Tone

## General Principles
- Lead with the ask or the update (don't bury it)
- Keep emails short — aim for "readable in 30 seconds"
- Use bullets for anything with 2+ items
- Be warm but professional; match the recipient's formality level

## Structure Template
```
Subject: [Action needed / FYI] Brief topic

Hi [Name],

[1-2 sentence context or update]

[Bullets if needed]:
- Item 1
- Item 2

[Clear ask or next step]

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Client Emails
- More formal; use "Dear" or "Hi [First name]" depending on relationship
- Always proofread for tone — avoid anything that reads as demanding
- Attach relevant materials and reference them in the body
- End with a clear next step and timeline

## Internal / Team Emails
- Can be more casual
- Use subject line prefixes: [FYI], [Action needed], [For review], [Decision needed]
- Tag people by name if there's a specific ask for them

## Meeting Follow-ups
```
Subject: Follow-up: [Meeting topic] — [Date]

Hi all,

Thanks for the discussion. Key takeaways:
- [Decision or alignment point 1]
- [Decision or alignment point 2]

Next steps:
- [Owner]: [Task] by [Date]
- [Owner]: [Task] by [Date]

Let me know if I missed anything.

Best,
[Name]
```
